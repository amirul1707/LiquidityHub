# LiquidityHub

Manage liquidity positions with confidence.

LiquidityHub is a decentralized finance (DeFi) platform designed to help liquidity providers discover, monitor, and optimize liquidity pool positions across multiple protocols.

The platform provides a unified dashboard for tracking liquidity performance, monitoring rewards, and evaluating risk exposure through advanced analytics.

---

## Vision

LiquidityHub aims to simplify liquidity management by providing transparent insights into pool performance, APR trends, and impermanent loss exposure.

Our mission is to help liquidity providers make informed decisions while improving capital efficiency across DeFi ecosystems.

---

## Key Features

### Pool Discovery

Discover liquidity pools across supported protocols and compare opportunities based on APR, volume, and liquidity depth.

### APR Analytics

Track historical and real-time APR performance to better understand reward generation and yield sustainability.

### Liquidity Monitoring

Monitor active positions, pool performance, and liquidity allocation from a single dashboard.

### Impermanent Loss Insights

Estimate potential impermanent loss exposure before entering or exiting liquidity positions.

### Portfolio Overview

View liquidity positions, rewards earned, and overall portfolio performance through a unified interface.
